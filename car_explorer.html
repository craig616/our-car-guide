Crash testing
Female safety — is it a concern?

Yes, and it's been a serious long-standing issue. Here's the honest picture and how our shortlist performs.

The problem: A landmark 2019 University of Virginia study found women are 73% more likely to be severely injured or die in a frontal crash than men in comparable accidents. Crash dummies have historically been modelled on a 50th-percentile male. That's a testing gap, not a biological one.

What Euro NCAP currently does

Female dummy in front passenger?Yes — since 2003
Female dummy as driver?Not yet
New US THOR-05F female dummyLaunched Dec 2025
Euro NCAP 2026/27 roadmapFemale driver dummy coming

The gap is closing fast. Euro NCAP's next protocol update will require equal protection testing across both sexes in all positions. Cars tested after 2027 will face this standard.
