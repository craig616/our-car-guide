Mortgage first: Speak to your broker before any loan application. A £150/month car loan can reduce your mortgage offer by £20–30k. If the mortgage is within 3 months, buy cash only.
Where to buy

✓ Official dealer (best)

  • Approved Used — car inspected, history checked
  • Warranty top-up included (12 months min)
  • Battery health checked on EVs
  • 30-day/1,000mi exchange (Hyundai)

~ Independent dealer

  • Consumer Rights Act 2015 still applies
  • Often cheaper than official
  • Get a battery health report yourself
  • Verify warranty carefully

~ Online / Private

  • Private sale: lowest price, least protection
  • Cazoo/Cinch: convenient, return policies
  • Always run HPI check (£20) on private
  • AutoTrader: best search tool
Negotiation: Always negotiate on total purchase price, not monthly payment. Dealers prefer monthly conversations — don't let them reframe the deal.

Legal & admin

Buyer protection · checks · ULEZ · what to do when

Consumer Rights Act 2015

  • Fault within 30 days: full refund right
  • Fault within 6 months: repair first, then refund
  • Applies to dealers — not private sellers

Before you buy

  • HPI Check (~£20) — no outstanding finance, not stolen
  • V5C logbook matches seller and car
  • MOT history — free at gov.uk/check-mot-history
  • Battery health report — ask dealer, want 85%+

After you buy

  • Register keeper change via DVLA within 2 weeks
  • Insurance active before driving off forecourt
  • Road tax — apply immediately, not automatic
  • Newham parking permit — newham.gov.uk (~£110/yr)

ULEZ & London rules

  • All EVs are ULEZ exempt — no charge ever
  • E12 Manor Park is within the ULEZ boundary
  • Petrol/diesel paying £12.50/day if non-compliant
  • Congestion Charge — EVs no longer exempt (£15/day if driving in zone)
